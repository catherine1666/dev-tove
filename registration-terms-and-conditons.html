Terminating your service

You may terminate your service with us and we may terminate your service, either party giving one month notice to the other in writing. If you are obtaining funding from the Gigabit Broadband Voucher Scheme there is a 12 month minimum term of service.

Your use of the service

The service can be used by you and other people (including guests) within your property. It is your responsibility to ensure that no-one else can in any way use your connection to the Tove Valley system. If you deploy access points which range beyond your property it is your responsibility to utilise encryption or other means to prevent connections from outside your property. You will be held responsible for any action a third party takes by accessing your broadband connection.
