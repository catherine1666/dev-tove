5 - IN YOUR HOUSE

TVB is not responsible for how you distribute the broadband service in your house and we do not provide WiFi routers as every house differs in layout and construction and will require appropriate equipment. We will give suggestions for suitable equipment depending upon your property and requirements. There is plenty of advice online and in our library.

6 - INSTALLATION PROCEDURE

When you have registered, we will contact you as soon as we can to arrange a survey of your premises, discuss your options and schedule your installation. When the installation has been completed and the service is being delivered, you will have 30 days to pay the membership fee of £100, the installation fees agreed and the yearly service fee.
