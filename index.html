Welcome to Tove Valley Broadband

Abthorpe Broadband Association Limited - a not-for-profit limited by guarantee company - is a volunteer run organisation and has been connecting houses to fibre-optic broadband with up to 1,000Mbps unrestricted symmetrical service to our Members.

Tove Valley Broadband or TVB as it has come to be known, brings a very fast 10,000Mbps symetrical fibre-optic internet connection into the villages and distributes connections to properties by fibre-optics or by radio.

Our Coverage

Tove Valley Superfast Broadband brings 10,000Mbps internet connection to villages in our area with fibre optics and radio.

Our area of coverage extends to the following villages in South Northamptonshire:

What it Costs

What it will cost (including VAT) to connect to our broadband (by radio or fibre) - These charges will be invoiced when installation is complete and service established:

  • £100 membership fee per household/premises. It is a requirement of our not-for-profit Association and pays for the system's infrastructure.
  • £90 installation fee for a radio connection at your premises. If you are moving into a house already connected to TVB by radio this cost may not be charged. OR
  • For a fibre-to-the premises connection, the installation charge depends upon a variety of issues and you must contact us to discuss your requirements. If you are moving into a house already connected to TVB by fibre this cost may not be charged.
  • £180 annual fee for domestic and commercial users. If 5 or more workstations are dependent upon an internet connection, we may charge £540 per annum.

What we do

We provide the service at an ethernet socket in your premises but do not provide WiFi routers or other internal devices simply because no one solution fits all circumstances. We will give suggestions for suitable equipment depending upon your property and requirements. Our library documents contain much of this information.

If you are a business with more than 5 people associated in any way with the internet use, you must contact us to discuss your requirements.

If you are looking for information about fixed (public) IP addresses and other services we can offer, please refer to our library document "Fixed Public IP addresses".
