What we do

Equipment

We provide the service at an ethernet socket in your premises but do not provide WiFi routers or other internal devices simply because no one solution fits all circumstances. We will give suggestions for suitable equipment depending upon your property and requirements.

Tove Valley Broadband supplies the broadband service connected via Customer Premise Equipment (CPE)(radio) or Optical Network Unit (ONU)(fibre) - which is powered through a power injector - a small (3" x 1.5" x 1") black or white device connected to your electricity mains - and finally an ethernet cable which you can plug into a router, your computer or a switch. Some ONUs are powered directly from the mains. TVB responsibility ends at the ethernet cable. If you were supplied new kit for your home or had existing equipment re-programmed, this would have been undertaken as a community service and carries no guarantees or commitments other than the replacement of faulty equpiment supplied by us.

Businesses

We can work with you if you are a business with more than 5 people associated in any way with the internet use. However, we must discuss discuss your requirements with you so please contact us if this is your situation.
